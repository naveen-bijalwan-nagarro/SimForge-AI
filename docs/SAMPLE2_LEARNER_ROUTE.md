## Why the earlier report showed zeroes

The three action bars are scored from committed events in a **live run**, not from the scenario prompt or from opening a tab. The displayed “No diagnosis was recorded before debrief” means the learner did not click **Submit diagnosis** before the run reached its horizon. **Complete run** now warns if a diagnosis, an Inspect action before a response, or a Repair action for the diagnosed system is missing. Choose **Cancel** to return to **Diagnose & act**, or **OK** to finish anyway. The warning checks for missing steps; it does not guarantee a correct diagnosis or full points. Once completed, the run cannot gain these points retrospectively; start a fresh learner run.

## Rehearsal sequence for the full practice score

1. Publish the reviewed draft and wait for its notification to appear automatically in the learner view; use **Refresh notifications** for an immediate fetch. Open the loaded mission, generate the linked synthetic dataset and take the three-question **starting check** before playback. The library, assignments and run views also refresh in place while visible. Their **Refresh library**, **Refresh progress** and **Refresh view** controls fetch immediately without advancing the exercise clock.
2. Start playback. The generated incident begins at simulated minute **5**. Pause at or shortly after minute 5, with enough time left for a four-minute repair to take effect. Inspect Operations and Evidence to decide which system failed first.
3. Open **Diagnose & act**. Commit **Investigate source signals** first. Opening Evidence alone does not count: the score requires this committed Inspect action after the incident starts and before any non-Inspect response.
4. In **Root-cause diagnosis**, select the **deployment** system and the cause matching the generated draft’s release configuration fault. Click **Submit diagnosis** before debrief. The cause options can be shuffled; read them rather than relying on position.
5. Commit **Repair the source** while at least four simulated minutes remain. This is the generated scenario’s generic repair action at the incident node; explain it aloud as the simulated recovery response, not as a literal production rollback control. The optional alternate-route action does not earn targeted-repair credit.
6. Click **Complete run**. If the missing-step warning appears, choose **Cancel** and record the intended actions before finishing. Answer all three post-check questions. The report then shows earned points for knowledge check (up to 50), correct diagnosis (25), investigation before response (15), and targeted repair (10). Incorrect post-check answers or a wrong diagnosis reduce the score honestly.

For a rehearsed demo, read each question rather than selecting by option position. The starting check asks for adjacent handoff records, evidence/risk/delay/cost before changing a workflow, and a same-seed no-action comparison. The post-check asks for evidence at the new queued handoff, a verified targeted response, and the limited conclusion that a simulated improvement holds under the exercise assumptions. These are contextual reasoning questions generated by the app from the scenario workflow; Codex does not write a custom deployment exam.

All service metrics and outcomes are synthetic and illustrative. The three-question check is a brief practice measure, not certification or proof of lasting knowledge transfer. The learner-side simulation makes no model call.
